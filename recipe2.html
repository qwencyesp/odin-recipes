Shrimp Scampi Recipe

This baked shrimp scampi comes out saucy and buttery, with a bright kick from Dijon mustard that sets it apart from the classic stovetop version.

Prep Time 10 minutes
Cook Time 15 minutes
Servings 4

What is Shrimp Scampi?

Shrimp scampi bake is a casserole-style take on classic shrimp scampi, where shrimp are tossed in a garlic, butter, white wine, and lemon sauce and then baked in the oven instead of sautéed on the stovetop. It’s usually served over or mixed with pasta or rice and topped with breadcrumbs or Parmesan for a golden, crispy finish. The result is a rich, savory, citrusy dish that’s easy to prepare in one baking dish and great for feeding a crowd.

How long to bake Shrimp Scampi

Shrimp scampi bake typically takes about 10 to 15 minutes at 400°F (200°C), just until the shrimp turn pink and opaque. If you're adding a breadcrumb or Parmesan topping, you can broil for an extra 1 to 2 minutes at the end to get it golden, but watch closely so the shrimp don't overcook.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup white wine
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons breadcrumbs

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Season the shrimp with salt and pepper. Toss with flour to coat.
  3. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the garlic and sauté for 1 minute.
  4. Add the shrimp and cook until they turn pink and opaque, about 2-3 minutes.
  5. Stir in the wine, lemon juice, and Dijon mustard. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  6. Reduce heat to low and stir in the cream. Cook until the sauce thickens, about 5 minutes.
  7. Transfer the shrimp and sauce to the prepared baking dish.
  8. Top with Parmesan cheese and breadcrumbs.
  9. Bake for 10-15 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown.
